About UPARI Consulting

UPARI Consulting is a specialist Oracle ERP Cloud advisory and implementation firm. We work exclusively with Oracle Fusion applications — bringing deep product knowledge, proven methodologies, and a disciplined delivery approach to every client engagement.

Our Practitioner Model

Our team has held senior roles at Oracle, Big 4 consulting firms, and global enterprises. We understand both the technology and the organisational complexity that comes with large-scale ERP transformation — and we know how to navigate it.

We do not operate as a volume-driven practice. Each engagement is staffed with senior consultants and managed directly by principals who are accountable for outcomes. You work directly with senior practitioners who have hands-on Oracle Cloud experience — not junior staff learning on the job.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to deliver high-quality implementation experience at optimal cost, in shorter duration — for every customer, every industry, and every scale.

We build transformations that are on time, within scope, and built to last. Our goal is to equip our clients with the systems, skills, and confidence to operate independently after go-live, rather than maintaining long-term advisory dependence.

Core Pillars of Our Practice

  • Practitioner-Led Teams: We are built by practitioners who have lived through complex deployments. We staff projects with experienced advisors who understand the integration of finance, supply chain, and operations.
  • Packaged Accelerators: We leverage a library of pre-built configuration setups, a Chart of Accounts design toolkit, and a repeatable Data Migration Engine to compress project timelines and reduce risk.
  • Independent Advocacy: We hold no software reseller agreements. Our advice remains fully independent, focused entirely on what matches your requirements and organizational interests.
