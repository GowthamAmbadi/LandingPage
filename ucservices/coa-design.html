Chart of Accounts Design

The Chart of Accounts (CoA) is the financial foundation of Oracle Fusion. UPARI provides structured CoA design workshops and proprietary validation tooling to ensure your account structure supports operational management, group consolidation, and statutory reporting — before a single journal is posted.

A well-designed Chart of Accounts is critical for standardising financial data across your organisation. It defines how transaction data is classified, recorded, and reported. In Oracle Fusion, a flexible, scalable segment structure is essential to accommodate future business growth, mergers, and changing reporting requirements without requiring costly redesigns.

What Is Chart of Accounts Design?

Chart of Accounts Design involves defining the segments, value sets, ledger configurations, and cross-validation rules that govern your financial transactions. UPARI's specialists work with your finance leadership to balance detail with usability, designing a structure that captures essential reporting dimensions without overcomplicating daily processing.

Our design methodology ensures seamless alignment between your general ledger, subledgers, and reporting tools like OTBI, Financial Reporting Studio, and SmartView. By setting up robust validation controls upfront, we prevent posting errors and streamline period-end reconciliations.

Who This Service Is Designed For

Our Chart of Accounts Design services are suited to organisations that:

  • Are implementing or upgrading to Oracle Fusion Cloud Financials
  • Experience difficulty with multi-entity consolidation or intercompany transactions
  • Have an overly complex, legacy account structure with redundant segments
  • Require multi-currency, multi-GAAP, or local statutory compliance reporting
  • Seek a standardised reporting structure across diverse business units
  • Want to optimize reporting dimensions for managers and executives
  • Are restructuring their business model or financial processes
  • Need to clean and align account structures prior to data migration

Our Design Approach

UPARI follows a proven, structured methodology to design and validate your new Chart of Accounts:

Phase 1 — Discovery & Workshops

We review your legacy CoA structure, operational reporting needs, and organizational hierarchy to establish core design principles and segment requirements.

Phase 2 — Segment Design & Validation

We model the segment structure, define value set rules, and configure cross-validation rules (CVRs) to enforce data integrity within Oracle Fusion.

Phase 3 — Mapping & Testing

We map your legacy account values, validate data translation rules, and run volume load tests in test environments to ensure compatibility and ease of close.
