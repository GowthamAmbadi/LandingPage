AI Agents in Oracle ERP

UPARI Consulting designs and deploys secure, custom AI agents to automate complex, multi-system workflows within Oracle ERP Cloud. Our agentic systems perform document reconciliation, transaction matching, exception resolution, and conversational reporting analysis.

Traditional ERP processes require extensive manual validation, data matching, and text analysis. AI Agents introduce secure, autonomous workflows that sit alongside Oracle Fusion APIs, retrieving documents, validating values against rules, resolving standard exceptions, and extracting conversational analytics.

What Are AI Agents in Oracle ERP?

AI Agents in Oracle ERP are secure, context-aware software agents that execute multi-step operations on behalf of your finance team. Using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and secure connection to Oracle REST APIs, these agents parse supplier invoices, match transaction lines, resolve ledger exceptions, and answer natural language reporting queries against OTBI structures.

We build these systems with a "human-in-the-loop" authorization model, ensuring that high-value transactions or sensitive ledger updates require explicit sign-off from a supervisor before execution.

Who This Service Is Designed For

Our AI Agents services are suited to organisations that:

  • Wish to automate high-volume reconciliation, statement matching, or invoice extraction tasks
  • Suffer from processing backlogs caused by complex subledger exception routing
  • Want to provide managers and executives with instant, voice/text-based access to OTBI reports
  • Seek to build custom, secure AI models that respect financial data security and role-based access rules
  • Need to cross-reference unstructured documents (SOPs, supplier emails) with Oracle ledger entries
  • Want to monitor transactional patterns dynamically to flag compliance anomalies or fraud risks
