Job Aids and Process Documentation

Well-written, role-specific process documentation is one of the most practical investments an organisation can make during an ERP implementation. UPARI produces job aids, SOPs, and quick reference guides tailored to your Oracle Fusion configuration — not generic vendor content.

Standard training materials explain *how* Oracle Fusion features work, but they don't cover *how your business* operates within the system. UPARI creates role-based SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) and job aids that map step-by-step tasks, business decisions, and approval rules, providing users with a clear guide to performing their daily jobs.

What Is Job Aids and Process Documentation?

Job Aids and Process Documentation is a user enablement service that translates your system configuration into plain-language, structured operational manuals. UPARI's documentation experts interview your functional teams and review system profiles to draft customized guides, incorporating actual system screenshots, approval matrices, and data standards.

We deliver documentation in digital formats designed for easy access, including quick reference cards for infrequent tasks and detailed SOPs that serve as a source of truth for audits.

Who This Service Is Designed For

Our Process Documentation services are suited to organisations that:

  • Are preparing for go-live on Oracle Fusion Cloud and need training materials
  • Experience user errors, processing delays, or high support ticket volume
  • Have unique or customized Oracle configurations that generic guides don't cover
  • Want to preserve operational knowledge and streamline onboarding for new hires
  • Need to document financial approval paths for internal or external auditors
  • Seek to standardize process execution across multiple global regions

Our Documentation Approach

We build custom documentation tailored to your exact system setup:

Phase 1 — Discovery & Role Mapping

We analyze your business roles, match them to Oracle Fusion access configurations, and prioritize processes requiring documentation.

Phase 2 — Content Creation

We draft detailed, step-by-step guides using annotated screen captures from your specific test or production environment.

Phase 3 — Review & Publishing

We run verification checks with users to ensure clarity, upload guides to your document portals, and train internal trainers.
