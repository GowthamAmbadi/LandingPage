Financial Services

Banking, insurance, asset management, and capital markets organisations

🏦

Financial services organisations face a uniquely demanding Oracle ERP environment — multi-entity structures, strict regulatory reporting obligations, complex intercompany transactions, and audit requirements that leave little room for configuration error. UPARI's practitioners have implemented Oracle Fusion Finance across banking, insurance, and investment management environments, and understand the difference between a technically correct implementation and one that survives a regulatory examination.

Common Challenges We Address

  • Month-end close cycles exceeding 10 days due to manual consolidation processes
  • Regulatory reporting — IFRS 9, IFRS 17, Solvency II — not embedded in the ERP
  • Multi-entity, multi-currency ledger complexity across jurisdictions
  • Intercompany eliminations managed outside Oracle in spreadsheets
  • Audit trails that do not meet internal or external examination standards
  • High supplier invoice volumes processed without automation or routing controls
  • Siloed systems creating reconciliation overhead between front and back office

What UPARI Delivers in This Sector

  • Chart of Accounts design aligned to regulatory and group reporting requirements
  • Intercompany and consolidation configuration within Oracle Fusion
  • Subledger accounting rule design for financial instrument transactions
  • Oracle Fusion AP automation — IDR, work queues, and approval workflows
  • Month-end close checklist design and period close task configuration
  • Integration between Oracle Fusion and front-office or treasury platforms
  • Post-go-live Day 2 support and quarterly Oracle update management

Healthcare

Hospital systems, health networks, payers, and healthcare services organisations

🏥

Healthcare organisations operate under financial and operational constraints unlike any other sector — fund accounting, grant management, complex workforce structures, and compliance obligations spanning financial, clinical, and procurement functions. UPARI understands that in healthcare, a poorly configured ERP is not just a finance problem; it has downstream consequences for procurement, workforce, and patient services.

Common Challenges We Address

  • Fund and grant accounting requirements not met by standard Oracle configuration
  • Fragmented procurement across clinical departments with no centralised visibility
  • Complex workforce costing — multiple pay rates, shift patterns, and cost allocations
  • Legacy systems (PeopleSoft, Lawson, Meditech) requiring migration or integration
  • High supplier invoice volumes stressing manual AP processes
  • Regulatory and audit reporting — CMS and HIPAA financial compliance obligations
  • Consolidation requirements across multiple legal entities within a health network

What UPARI Delivers in This Sector

  • Oracle Fusion Finance configuration for fund and grant accounting structures
  • Procurement Cloud setup for clinical and non-clinical supply categories
  • AP automation — IDR and work queue management for high invoice volumes
  • Integration between Oracle Fusion and clinical or payroll systems
  • Chart of Accounts design for multi-entity health network reporting requirements
  • Process documentation and job aids tailored to healthcare finance teams
  • EBS to Oracle Fusion Cloud migration for legacy health system customers

Higher Education

Universities, colleges, research institutions, and academic bodies

🎓

Higher education institutions face a distinctive financial management challenge — combining the rigour of a regulated public or charitable entity with the operational scale of a mid-to-large enterprise. Research grant management, tuition revenue recognition, complex fund accounting, and the need to serve multiple internal stakeholder groups simultaneously make Oracle Fusion configuration in this sector genuinely specialised work. UPARI brings both the technical depth and sector understanding to deliver it correctly.
